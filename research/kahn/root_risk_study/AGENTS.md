# Root Risk Study

- This is Codex-authored offline research, not Kahn runtime policy or approval
  to dispatch, build, or deploy. Keep strategy and profile files read-only.
- Reconstruct live rails from the complete runtime log prefix. Campaign changes
  do not reset LL; evidence warmup/restart boundaries do. Never combine reused
  rail IDs across evidence epochs.
- Freeze entry inputs before inspecting later outcomes. A later rail cannot
  become a pre-entry sponsor, and a later failure cannot select a prior sponsor.
- Directional location and overlapping coverage are diagnostics, not proof of
  causal pairing. Do not silently choose the nearest/newest or merge distinct
  rails into an invented risk owner.
- Preserve actual broker fills separately from LL mid-price observations.
  A policy counterfactual is not a fill or a complete strategy replay.
- Future plans should bind risk ownership explicitly. Do not infer a hard stop
  from root_stop_ticks, widen to the probe box, or change LL thresholds here.
