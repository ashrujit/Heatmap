# Scale Cycle Study

- This is a causal, offline research comparison, not a strategy implementation.
  Keep its data, models, outputs, and tests under research ownership.
- Reuse the shared capture loader and the linked C# evidence engine. The older
  LL adapter has different default cluster/failure settings.
- Price and GEX joins must use information available by the decision timestamp.
  A snapshot is unavailable until both collection and API timestamps permit it.
- Future prices are outcome labels only. Never use them to select a root,
  qualify a repair, label an input feature, or choose a gamma category.
- Keep rejected/failed roots and stale-data cases. First-add comparisons use
  identical hypothetical roots; these are not historical fills or full P&L.
- Censor a root at an evidence gap. Do not carry its vanished proof forward.
- Preserve the original studies and notes. Explain methodological changes in
  a new findings document and link it from the refined backlog.
- Full-campaign NQ research keeps GEX and geographic order zones out of the
  comparison. Do not mistake a first-add count for whole-move participation.
- Keep each add's active/pending sponsor lineage and capacity visible. Promoting
  a failed pending rail is a defect to expose, not a favorable hold outcome.
- Retaining proof and relaxing proof-versus-repair geometry are separate changes.
  Label bundled prototype rules and retain the stricter range comparison.
- Shared ES/NQ episodes must retain challenged versus untested members and actual
  covered intervals. Band fragmentation alone cannot create extra permissions.
- A breached group can rebuild under a still-live repair; never resurrect its
  failed IDs. Keep unused resolved repair separate from a new superseding attack.
- Separate inventory construction from later maintenance. Reaching an add budget
  must not decide whether newer evidence changes risk on existing inventory.
- Label group-risk exits and BBO BE as experiments. A window-end mark is not paid
  harvest, and tranche reductions do not select a cheaper broker average price.
- Frozen stress runs pin the earlier observer's source hash. Record weaknesses
  rather than retuning the model on a requested example and calling it a test.
  Its scoped `.gitattributes` entry disables newline conversion so checkout
  cannot alter the frozen bytes under Windows `core.autocrlf=true`.
- OWN time is confirmation, not candidate formation. Formation/source-side
  metadata can diagnose association but cannot backdate ownership permission.
- Far-edge repair is not LL TEST. Observe prices against claims already known;
  do not retroactively test new OWN against earlier trades. Keep repair onset
  separate from formal opposing failure and root-risk failure.
- Equivalent fragment tests preserve original claim identity/edges. Unlabelled
  splits change far edges; report sensitivity rather than invent parentage or
  claim detector-setting invariance.
- A resolved repair cannot absorb a new far-edge attack merely because its old
  pre-attack extreme was not exceeded. Test this separately from delayed proof.
