Held demand plus failed supply above. Held supply plus failed demand below. Last repair failure.

Core distinction

New Band Vs Failed Opposite Structure

Color carries origin: blue/orange means a fresh lean claim; green/red with `C` means the opposite side was consumed into this side.

Weaker: fresh same-side band

A new demand band above a low only says buyers appeared. It can be consumed into supply and used to retest the low.

new demand above = unproven

Stronger: opposite side fails

Supply failing above a held low says sellers had a defense point and lost it. That upgrades the low.

held demand + failed supply = credibility

Do not confuse these

VOD Is Not Auction Failure

VOD chaos: the book is unstable. It can precede failure, continuation, exhaustion, or repair.

Action: raise attention, maybe partial at target, but wait for structure before declaring failure.

  • VOD plus no repair = do not force exit from a working trade.
  • VOD followed by LF/HF/RF or consumed opposite ownership = real decision point.
  • At objective: ask whether same-side continuation fails, not whether the tape looked scary.

First Paint

Useful information, not a trade. The first band tells you where to watch whether the claim holds.

Retested Structure

A band that gets tested and holds gives you a nearby invalidation point. This is where confidence can increase.

Pattern 1

Last Buyer Repair Fails Under Old Supply

Read: buyers created support for the test and could not defend it. Sellers may already have enough proof to press.

490 example: C demand 489-491 held briefly, then failed. No new supply was required for seller credibility.

Pattern 2

Held Low Plus Failed Supply Above

Read: the low is not upgraded by fresh higher demand alone. It is upgraded when sellers fail above it.

336 example: demand held, then 401 and 491 supply failed. That confirmed more than a fresh green band would.

Pattern 4

Fast Auction: Direction Clear, Permission Missing

When price travels thinly and temporary demand/supply is run over, the direction can be obvious while the trade location is poor.

  • Acceptable: initial open reclaim, failed repair, or tested edge with nearby invalidation.
  • Not enough: "sellers are selling" after price is already away from structure.
  • If the only plan is "probe and abandon on first trouble", location must be close enough for tiny risk.
Probe Rules

First LF/HF Or First Band

  • Probe only when invalidation is close enough, often 4-8 ticks.
  • Do not call it confirmation until the band is tested and holds.
  • Ask what opposite structure failed after the hold.
Add Rules

Do Not Average A Feeling

  • Add short after supply retests and survives, or after buyer repair fails.
  • Add long after demand retests and survives, or after seller repair fails.
  • Each add needs its own nearby wrong-location.
Exit Rules

Target Is A Question

  • VOD near target means watch closely, not automatic exit.
  • Exit if opposite repair holds or same-side continuation fails.
  • If no repair appears and no demand/supply survives against you, trailing is valid.
Language to say out loud

Pre-Trade Script

Before entry

"This is a claim, not permission. What test am I trading? Where is it wrong?"

"Am I inside grey, or outside with a clean edge?"

"If I need first trouble to exit, is the risk tiny enough?"

Before exit

"Is this VOD only, or did repair/failure follow?"

"Did the last repair fail, or did it hold?"

"Did opposite supply/demand fail above/below my area?"
