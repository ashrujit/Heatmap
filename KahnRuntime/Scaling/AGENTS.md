# Shared Repair Core

- This module is pure and deterministic. No clock reads, broker API, I/O, plan
  file mutation, or GEX-derived execution authority belongs here.
- `CampaignSession` and the worker adapter supply complete samples and actual
  cumulative fills. Do not bypass that adapter with per-transition add decisions.
- TEST is a valid broad repair onset. Do not restore the rejected far-edge gate
  through a renamed distance, excursion, or band-count threshold.
- Price-path association is not proof coverage. Only union actual claim ticks
  into a proof group; holes never become owned prices.
- A claim identity includes source and epoch. Failed IDs are terminal; missing
  formation is unknown. Incomplete/out-of-order observations suspend authority.
- Observation is independent of WATCH, capacity, and policy veto. Permission
  requires typed failure of every associated opposing claim, never proximity
  substitution, a price-only failure, or a mandatory extra post-failure HOLD.
- A new attack supersedes a resolved episode. Later proof must contact the
  repair's frozen causal area; an unrelated extension cannot inherit old failure.
- Reservation is not a fill. First partial fill consumes one episode and queues
  one group; remaining fills cannot cause a second sponsor promotion or add.
- Sponsor membership is frozen at fill. Only its recorded qualifying members
  can keep it alive. Missing epoch state is unknown, not typed failure.
