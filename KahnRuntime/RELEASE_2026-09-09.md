# Root Ownership Release - 2026-09-09

The root-risk update binds schema-2 probes to exact live LL owner identities,
separately from their entry triggers. Complete engine snapshots retain ownership
across campaign reloads; confirmed owner failure remains actionable through
pending orders, partial/late fills and reconciliation lag. Neighbor failure is
not owner failure, and TEST remains distinct from FAIL.

This increment intentionally blocks counter-claim-only entries until a causal
pair resolver is validated. Direct live same-side OWN/HOLD remains supported.
The optional `risk.max_root_entry_distance_ticks` defaults to unset; it does not
enable the blocked entry path, impose a hard stop or tighten to later rails.
Correct ownership alone can still allow a wide probe. Retry limits, terminal BE
retirement, LL thresholds and one-behind group sponsorship are unchanged.
See [implementation scope and limitations](ROOT_RISK_IMPLEMENTATION_2026-09-09.md).
